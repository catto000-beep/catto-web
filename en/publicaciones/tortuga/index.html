🐢 The Logo Turtle

The language many of us learned to program with: a turtle with a pen that obeys commands such as forward 100 or right 90. With repetitions and procedures that call themselves you get all the way to fractals. It accepts commands in English and also in Spanish, as in LogoWriter.

Program

The Draw button clears the screen and runs the program. The command center continues from where the turtle was left and knows the procedures in the program.
x 0y 0heading 0°pen down

Worked examples

Ordered from the simplest to the hardest. Each one explains the new idea that appears; the button loads it into the program and draws it. Afterwards, try changing the numbers and see what happens: that is how you learn Logo.

The commands

The screen is 600 steps wide, with the origin at the center. Heading 0 points up and increases clockwise. Commands can be written with or without accents, in lowercase or uppercase, and separated by spaces or on different lines.

Calculations are written as in mathematics: :side / 2, :n - 1, 360 / :sides. A minus sign attached to the number and separated from what precedes it, as in dragon :n -1, is a negative number, not a subtraction. Whatever follows a ; is a comment.
