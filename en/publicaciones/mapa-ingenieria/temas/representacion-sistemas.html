    Representation of signals and systems

    Four ways of describing the same thing, none of them truer than the others. Each problem is easy to solve from one of them, and a good part of the craft consists of choosing where to look from.

    Differential equation Transfer function State variables Difference equation z-plane

    01The same system, four descriptions

    An LTI system can be described in four different ways, and all four say exactly the same thing. They are not alternatives you have to choose between: they are points of view, and each problem is easier to solve from one of them.

    RepresentationFormGood for
    Differential equation \( \sum a_k \dfrac{d^k y}{dt^k} = \sum b_k \dfrac{d^k x}{dt^k} \) Starting from the circuit: it is what Kirchhoff’s laws and the component laws give
    Impulse response \( h(t) \), with \( y = x * h \) Seeing the time response; it is measured by striking the system
    Transfer function \( H(s) = \dfrac{Y(s)}{X(s)} \) Cascades, stability, filter design
    State variables \( \dot{\mathbf{q}} = A\mathbf{q} + B x,\quad y = C\mathbf{q} + D x \) Systems with several inputs and outputs; numerical simulation

    The most frequent path in practice goes from the first to the third. You set up the circuit, obtain the differential equation, transform it —so that derivatives become powers of \( s \)— and are left with a ratio of polynomials that is analyzed with poles and zeros.

    \[ \frac{d}{dt} \;\longrightarrow\; s \qquad\qquad \frac{d^k}{dt^k} \;\longrightarrow\; s^k \] With zero initial conditions. That is all the magic: a differential equation becomes algebraic.

    02All four faces at once

    So that the equivalence does not stay in a table, it helps to see the four move together. The lab takes a second-order system —the most common in electronics— and shows what happens to each representation when a parameter is changed.

    Lab · one thing, four ways of saying it

    Move the parameters and all four descriptions change at the same time: the equation, the poles, the impulse response and the frequency response.

    03The discrete version

    Everything repeats with square brackets. The differential equation becomes a difference equation, which is also directly programmable:

    \[ y[n] = \sum_{k=0}^{M} b_k\,x[n-k] \;-\; \sum_{k=1}^{N} a_k\,y[n-k] \] Each term is a stored sample, multiplied by a coefficient. That is, literally, the filter’s program.

    And the transfer function is obtained with the same trick, except that now the delay of one sample becomes \( z^{-1} \):

    \[ x[n-k] \;\longrightarrow\; z^{-k}X(z) \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad H(z) = \frac{\sum b_k z^{-k}}{1 + \sum a_k z^{-k}} \]
    How to go from one to another, in practice

    From the equation to \( H \): transform and solve. From \( H \) to the impulse response: inverse transform, which with residues means adding one term per pole. From \( h \) to \( H \): transform. And from \( H \) to the state variables: choose a set of variables —typically the capacitor voltages and the inductor currents— and write their derivatives. That last path is the one any circuit simulator uses internally.

    04Signals also have several representations

    RepresentationWhen it is useful
    \( x(t) \), the waveformSeeing amplitudes, times, edges
    Fourier seriesPeriodic signals: which harmonics they have
    Fourier transformIsolated signals: how much bandwidth they occupy
    Laplace transformTransients and initial conditions
    Samples \( x[n] \)Anything that is going to pass through a processor

    The fact that there are so many is not excessive: each one makes visible something that the others hide. The waveform says nothing about the bandwidth, and the spectrum does not show whether the signal clips. A good part of the craft consists of choosing where to look from.

    05In the lab

    Activity 1 · From the circuit to the four forms

    For a series RLC with the output across the capacitor, set up the differential equation, obtain \( H(s) \), inverse transform to get \( h(t) \) and write the state equations taking \( v_C \) and \( i_L \) as variables. Verify that all four describe the same circuit.

    Activity 2 · Measure h(t) and deduce H

    Measure the step response of the RLC, differentiate it to obtain \( h(t) \), extract \( \sigma \) and \( \omega \) from the envelope and the period, and reconstruct \( H(s) \). Compare with the one calculated from the component values.

    Activity 3 · Programming the difference equation

    Discretize the same filter and program its difference equation on a microcontroller. Compare its step response with that of the analog circuit.

    06Common mistakes

    • Forgetting the zero initial conditions when replacing \( d/dt \) by \( s \). With nonzero initial conditions, extra terms appear.
    • Confusing \( H(s) \) with \( H(j\omega) \). The latter is the former evaluated on the imaginary axis.
    • Believing that one representation is more “true”. All four describe the same system.
    • Mixing up \( z^{-1} \) and \( s \). One is a delay of one sample; the other, a derivative.
    • Choosing the state variables badly. It is best to take those that store energy: capacitor voltages and inductor currents.

    07Self-assessment

    What are the four representations of an LTI system?

    Differential equation, impulse response, transfer function and state variables.

    What replaces \( d/dt \) when transforming?

    The variable \( s \), assuming zero initial conditions.

    What does \( z^{-1} \) represent?

    A delay of one sample. It is the memory element of any digital filter.

    How do you go from \( H(s) \) to \( h(t) \)?

    By inverse transforming, which with residues means adding one exponential term per pole.

    What should be taken as state variables in a circuit?

    Those that store energy: capacitor voltages and inductor currents.

    Why are there so many representations of a signal?

    Because each one makes visible something that the others hide: the waveform does not show the bandwidth, and the spectrum does not show whether it clips.
