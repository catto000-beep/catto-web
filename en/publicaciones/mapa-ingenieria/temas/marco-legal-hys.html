    Legal framework of occupational health and safety

    A single workshop answers to a law of 1972, a decree of 1979, resolutions that change every few years and the inspection of its province. Finding your way means knowing at which level to look.

    Law 19,587 Decree 351/79 Law 24,557 SRT resolutions ILO Conventions

    01The rules in order

    The protection of health at work in Argentina has several levels. At the top is the Constitution: Article 14 bis guarantees dignified and equitable working conditions, and Article 75, paragraph 22, gives constitutional rank to international treaties. Below come the laws of Congress, their regulatory decrees and the resolutions of the enforcement authority. Technical standards, such as those of IRAM (Argentine Institute for Standardization and Certification) or the AEA (Argentine Electrotechnical Association) regulations, are voluntary unless a mandatory rule incorporates them. The general structure of the system was covered in the Argentine regulatory system, and labor law in labor law, in Legislation.

    Laws and resolutions are amended frequently. What follows is a map to find your bearings; before applying a rule, consult its current text in InfoLEG or in the Boletín Oficial (Official Gazette).

    02Law 19,587 and its regulations

    Law 19,587 on Hygiene and Safety at Work, of 1972, sets out the principles: the employer must take the measures to protect the life and integrity of workers, and the worker must comply with the rules and use the protective equipment. Its regulations detail the requirements by activity:

    RuleScope
    Decree 351/79General regulation: establishments, electrical installations, fire protection, contaminants, noise, lighting, occupational hygiene and safety services and occupational medicine
    Decree 911/96Construction industry
    Decree 617/97Agricultural activity
    Decree 249/07Mining activity

    The Superintendency of Occupational Risks (SRT) updates the technical values by resolution. Some of those frequently used in electronics: 295/03, with the reference values for physical and chemical agents; 85/12 and 84/12, with the protocols for measuring noise and lighting; 900/15, on grounding measurement, and 299/11, on the supply of personal protective equipment.

    03The Occupational Risks Law

    Law 24,557, of 1995, organized the system of prevention and compensation. Every employer must take out cover with an occupational risk insurer (ART), which provides medical and cash benefits in the event of an accident or an occupational disease, and which also has prevention obligations: advising the employer and monitoring compliance. The SRT supervises the system.

    • It covers work accidents, those that occur on the way between home and work (in itinere) and the occupational diseases on the official list.
    • Law 26,773, of 2012, reorganized compensation, and Law 27,348, of 2017, established the prior step through the medical commissions.

    04The provincial level

    The provinces retain the police power over labor: their labor authorities inspect establishments, draw up citations and impose penalties for breaches of the hygiene and safety rules. Coordination between the Nation and the provinces was organized in the Federal Labor Pact, approved by Law 25,212. Some activities also have local rules, such as those on the licensing of establishments or on fire prevention in each municipality.

    05The international level

    The International Labour Organization (ILO) adopted Convention 155 on occupational safety and health, of 1981, and Convention 187 on the promotional framework, of 2006. Argentina ratified them through Law 26,693, of 2011. In 2022, the ILO incorporated a safe and healthy working environment into its fundamental principles and rights.

    On the voluntary side, the ISO 45001 standard, of 2018, defines an occupational health and safety management system that companies can have certified. It does not replace the law: it presupposes it.

    07Common mistakes

    • Citing a resolution without checking whether it has been amended. SRT resolutions are updated frequently.
    • Believing that the ART replaces the employer’s prevention. The duty to prevent remains with the employer.
    • Taking an IRAM or ISO standard as mandatory in itself. It is so only if a legal rule incorporates it or a contract requires it.
    • Forgetting the in itinere accident. It is also covered.

    08Self-assessment

    Which decree regulates Law 19,587 for industry in general?

    Decree 351/79.

    Who provides the benefits in the event of a work accident?

    The employer’s occupational risk insurer.

    Which ILO conventions on safety and health did Argentina ratify in 2011?

    Nos. 155 and 187, through Law 26,693.

    Who inspects compliance with the rules in an establishment?

    The provincial labor authority, and the ART and the SRT within the occupational risks system.
