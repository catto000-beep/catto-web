    Labor law

    The same task, done by an employed engineer or by one who invoices as a freelancer, is subject to completely different rules. What decides which applies is the reality of the relationship, not what the paper says.

    Employment Contract Act Employment relationship Severance Collective bargaining agreements Occupational risks

    01Employee or independent contractor

    An engineer can work in two very different ways as far as the law is concerned. In an employment relationship (relación de dependencia), they make their work available to an employer who organizes and directs it, in exchange for pay: the Ley de Contrato de Trabajo (Employment Contract Act, Law 20,744) and the applicable collective bargaining agreement govern. As an independent contractor, they provide services or works to their clients on their own account and at their own risk, issue invoices, and the Civil and Commercial Code applies.

    What decides which is which is not the name of the contract but the reality of the relationship. Subordination is the central indicator: who sets the schedule, the place and the manner of the work, what tools are used, whether there is exclusivity, whether the income is fixed and monthly. Article 23 of the Act presumes that providing services for another is an employment contract, unless proven otherwise; the 2024 reform (Law 27,742) narrowed that presumption when professional services are contracted with invoicing and bank payment, but the principle of the primacy of reality (substance over form) remains in force.

    Rules that change

    Argentine labor law underwent major changes in 2024. The figures on this page follow the text of the Employment Contract Act with those reforms, but for a real case you have to read the current text and the applicable collective bargaining agreement, and consult a labor lawyer.

    02The principles of labor law

    • Protection: the worker is the weaker party to the contract, and the law compensates for it. If there is doubt about how to interpret a rule, the one most favorable to the worker applies (Article 9).
    • Non-waivability (Article 12): any waiver of the rights granted by the law and by collective agreements is void, even if the worker signs it.
    • Primacy of reality: what happens counts, not what the papers say.
    • Continuity: in case of doubt, the contract is presumed to be of indefinite duration.

    04The end of the relationship and severance pay

    The contract ends by the worker’s resignation, by agreement, by dismissal for just cause —a serious breach (“injuria”) that makes it impossible to continue the relationship— or by dismissal without cause. In the latter case the employer must pay three main items:

    ItemArticleHow it is calculated
    Seniority severance pay245One month of the best normal and regular monthly pay of the last year for each year of service or fraction greater than three months; never less than one month; the base is capped according to the collective agreement
    Omitted notice231 and 232If advance notice was not given: one month’s salary with up to 5 years of seniority, two months with more
    Integration of the month of dismissal233If the dismissal is without notice before the last day of the month, the days remaining to complete it

    05Occupational risks, health and safety

    Every employer must contract a workers’ compensation insurer (aseguradora de riesgos del trabajo, ART; Law 24,557), which covers accidents and occupational diseases. And the employer must comply with the health and safety conditions of Law 19,587 and its implementing Decree 351/79; in construction, Decree 911/96 also applies. For an electronic engineer this has two sides: as the employer of a workshop or of a crew of installers, they are responsible for ensuring compliance; and as a professional, they are often the one who designs the electrical protections, the switchboards and the safe work procedures.

    08Common mistakes

    • Disguising an employment relationship with invoices. The reality of the relationship prevails.
    • Having someone sign a waiver of rights. It is void.
    • Believing that seniority counts only in whole years. A fraction of more than three months counts as a year.
    • Forgetting the workers’ compensation insurer. Without one, the employer is directly liable for accidents.
    • Calculating with old rules. The 2024 reform changed the probationary period and other points.

    09Self-assessment

    What central indicator distinguishes an employment relationship?

    Subordination: the employer organizes and directs the work.

    With 7 years and 2 months of seniority, how many months of seniority severance pay are due?

    Seven: the additional two months do not exceed three months.

    How long is the notice period with 8 years of seniority?

    Two months, because it exceeds five years.

    Which law requires contracting a workers’ compensation insurer?

    Law 24,557.
