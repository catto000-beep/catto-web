    Legislation on works

    A telecommunications installation in a public hospital is contracted, carried out and accepted under rules that have existed since 1947. Knowing them is part of the trade.

    Public works Tendering Direction of works Technical representative AEA regulations

    01Public works

    Law 13,064, of 1947, defines national public works as any construction, work or industrial service carried out with funds of the National Treasury. It remains in force, with its implementing regulations, and each province has its own law with a similar structure (in the province of Buenos Aires, Law 6021). A telecommunications network for a public hospital, a road signaling system or the electrical installation of a school are public works.

    What distinguishes it from a private construction project is that the State cannot freely choose whom to contract with or negotiate the terms: it has to follow a procedure that guarantees equality among bidders, publicity and the best offer for the public interest.

    02How it is contracted

    The rule is the public tender (licitación pública); the private tender and direct award are exceptions that the law lists, such as low amounts or emergencies. The typical sequence:

    1. Design and tender specifications: the agency prepares the design, the official budget and the specifications —general conditions, particular conditions and technical specifications—.
    2. Call and publicity: it is published with the advance notice set by the rules.
    3. Bids: bidders submit their proposal with a bid bond; the bids are opened in a public ceremony.
    4. Evaluation and award: the contract is awarded to the most advantageous bid, which is not always the cheapest.
    5. Contract: the awardee posts a performance bond and signs.
    Contracting systemHow it is paidWhen it is suitable
    Lump sum (ajuste alzado)A global price for the whole workComplete and well-defined design

    03Execution: who is who

    RoleRepresentsWhat it does
    Site inspection (inspección de obra)The client (the State)Checks that the design and the contract are complied with, approves the work and issues service orders
    Technical representativeThe contracting companyA licensed professional who answers technically for the execution and signs the request notes
    DesignerWhoever commissioned the designAnswers for the design and its calculations

    Formal communication is done in writing, in two books: the book of service orders (órdenes de servicio), from the inspection to the contractor, and the book of request notes (notas de pedido), from the contractor to the inspection. What was not recorded in those books is very hard to claim afterward. The work is paid for with periodic payment certificates based on the measured progress, from which a percentage is withheld as a retention fund (fondo de reparo) until acceptance.

    The work ends with two acts: provisional acceptance, when it is completed, which opens a warranty period in which the contractor corrects any defects that appear, and final acceptance, when that period expires, which releases the bonds and the retention fund.

    In long contracts with inflation, prices are updated under a regime of price redetermination, which at the national level is regulated by Decree 691/2016.

    04Private construction

    In private construction, the work contract of the Civil and Commercial Code applies, and the parties negotiate the terms. But the State is still present through the municipal police power: the building code requires a building permit with drawings signed by licensed professionals, inspections during construction and a final inspection in order to authorize occupancy. The roles are similar to those in public works: the construction supervisor represents the client, the builder executes and their technical representative answers for them.

    07Activities

    Activity 1 · Read a real tender specification

    Find on the national or a provincial public procurement portal a tender for an electronic installation, and identify the contracting system, the bonds, the deadlines and the technical requirements.

    Activity 2 · A service order

    Write a service order requesting a change in the location of a rack, and the request note with which the contractor responds, indicating the additional cost.

    Activity 3 · The municipal permit

    Find out what the local building code requires to authorize an antenna on the roof of a building.

    08Common mistakes

    • Bidding without reading the complete specification. What was not priced but the specification required is built anyway.
    • Accepting verbal changes from the inspection. Without a service order, the additional cost is hard to collect.
    • Confusing provisional and final acceptance. The bond and the retention fund are only released with the final one.
    • Negotiating with the State as with a private party. The procedure and the specification govern.

    09Self-assessment

    Which law regulates national public works?

    Law 13,064, of 1947.

    What is the difference between the site inspection and the technical representative?

    The inspection represents the client and oversees; the technical representative represents the contractor and answers for the execution.

    Which contracting system is suitable when quantities are uncertain?

    The unit-price system.

    What is released with final acceptance?

    The bonds and the retention fund.
