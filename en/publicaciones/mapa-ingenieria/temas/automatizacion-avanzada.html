    Advanced measurement automation systems

    With a Cpk of 1, three out of every thousand power supplies fall out of specification. With 1.33 and a centered process, about sixty-three per million. The test system not only decides which unit passes: it measures how good the process is.

    PXI and LXI IVI drivers Test sequencer Cp and Cpk Yield

    01From the bench instrument to the test system

    The measurement automation of Measurements I connected bench instruments to a computer with SCPI commands. In production, where thousands of devices are tested per day, that is not enough: compact, fast and easy-to-maintain systems are needed. There are three main modular platforms:

    PlatformIdeaAdvantage
    PXIInstruments as modules inside a chassis with a PCI Express bus, shared synchronization and triggersSpeed and density: dozens of instruments in one chassis
    LXIStandalone instruments over Ethernet, with a web interface and synchronization through IEEE 1588Distributed instruments, no chassis
    AXIeHigh-performance modules in a larger chassisHigh-end instruments, such as many-channel oscilloscopes

    On top of the hardware a layer of interchangeable drivers is used, such as IVI, which present a common interface per instrument class: a program written for one multimeter works with another from a different manufacturer without changes.

    02Sequencers and parallel testing

    A test sequencer organizes the test of a product as a list of steps, each with its measurement, its limits and its action on failure, and stores the results with the serial number. The same measurement code is reused across different products. To increase the number of units tested per hour, testing is done in parallel: several units at once, sharing the expensive instruments and using the cheap ones in parallel. The bottleneck is usually the shared instrument, and the design of the system consists of balancing the times.

    03Process capability

    The test system decides whether each unit passes or not, but the accumulated data say something more: how well the manufacturing process meets the specification. If the measured quantity has mean \( \mu \) and standard deviation \( \sigma \), and the specification runs from \( LSL \) to \( USL \):

    \[ C_p = \frac{USL - LSL}{6\sigma} \] \[ C_{pk} = \frac{\min(USL - \mu,\ \mu - LSL)}{3\sigma} \] \( C_p \) compares the width of the specification with the spread; \( C_{pk} \) also takes into account the shift of the mean. With \( C_{pk} = 1 \), 0.27% falls outside the specification; with 1.33, about 63 parts per million if the process is centered (about 30 if it is shifted and only the tail nearest one limit counts); with 1.67, fewer than one. The automotive industry usually demands 1.33 or more.

    04In the laboratory

    Activity 1 · A sequence

    Program in Python a test sequence for a laboratory power supply with a multimeter and an electronic load over LXI or USB: no-load voltage, load regulation and ripple, with limits and logging of results.

    Activity 2 · Real Cpk

    Measure the resistance of 50 1% resistors from the same bag and calculate Cp and Cpk with respect to their tolerance.

    Activity 3 · Test time

    Measure the time of each step of the sequence and propose how to reduce it: fewer averages, range changes, parallel testing.

    05Common mistakes

    • Looking only at Cp. A narrow but shifted process has a good Cp and a bad Cpk.
    • Calculating the capability with few samples. The estimated standard deviation varies a lot; tens or hundreds of units are needed.
    • Forgetting the test uncertainty. It adds to the spread of the process.
    • Writing the test tied to an instrument model. Interchangeable drivers avoid rewriting it when a device is changed.

    06Self-assessment

    Specification of 10 ± 0.3 mA, mean 10.1 mA and \( \sigma = 0.05\ \text{mA} \). What are Cp and Cpk?

    \( C_p = 0.6/0.3 = 2 \); \( C_{pk} = 0.2/0.15 = 1.33 \).

    What fraction falls outside the specification with \( C_{pk} = 1 \) and the process centered?

    0.27%.

    What advantage does LXI have over PXI?

    The instruments can be distributed over a network, with no chassis, and are synchronized through IEEE 1588.

    What are IVI drivers for?

    So that the test program does not depend on the instrument model within a given class.
