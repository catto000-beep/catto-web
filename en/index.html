    Electronics, from technician to engineer

    Two complete curricula developed topic by topic: the Electronics Technician program (years 4–7 of technical secondary school) and the Electronic Engineering degree at the UTN (Argentina's National Technological University). Theory, animated diagrams, worked examples, lab practice and self-assessment.

    Tools and publications

    Calculators, simulators and notes that go along with the subjects. They run in the browser, with nothing to install.

    🔬

    Instrument simulator

    A lab bench with nine instruments: two oscilloscopes, a spectrum analyzer, two generators, a multiplier, an adder and two filters. BNC cables are connected by dragging, and you can set up and compare double sideband, AM and the two single-sideband variants.

    Interactive Electronics Modulation
    Enter the bench →     🎨

    Resistor calculator

    Get a resistor's value from its color code. For 4 and 5 bands, with the color table and the calculation step by step.

    Interactive Electronics
    Open calculator →     🔢

    SI converter and symbols

    Convert between International System prefixes (pico, nano, micro, kilo…), decode the capacitor code and copy Greek and electronics symbols.

    Interactive Electronics
    Open tool →     🔀

    Digital logic

    Interactive course: binary numbers, logic gates (with 40XX ICs), a circuit builder with truth table, Karnaugh map and simplification, sequential logic and the datasheets of the ICs.

    Interactive Electronics
    Open course →     ⏱️

    LM555 simulator

    Astable and monostable: choose R and C and watch the capacitor charge and discharge next to the timing chart, with the equations, reverse design and the datasheet.

    Interactive Electronics
    Open simulator →     🔋

    Regulated power supply with the LM317

    From mains AC to a stable, adjustable DC: see how the signal is transformed at each stage, with the equations, the circuit and the datasheet.

    Interactive Electronics
    Open simulator →     🔺

    Operational amplifiers

    From the fundamentals to the instrumentation amplifier: each configuration with its schematic, its equation and the step-by-step mathematical derivation of the four basic amplifiers.

    Interactive Electronics
    Open card →     🎛️

    Bipolar transistors

    From the transistor effect to signal amplifiers: characteristic curves, load line, the transistor as a switch and as an amplifier, biasing, regulated power supplies, common emitter and common collector, and Darlington. With live diagrams and calculations.

    Interactive Electronics
    Open card →     📡

    MC1496 · Product modulator

    The classic balanced modulator: what it means to multiply two signals, DSB-SC versus AM, the IC inside and the circuit of Figure 27 of its datasheet, with live oscilloscopes and spectra.

    Vector study of AC

    Where the sine wave comes from, starting with a rotating vector, peak and RMS values, how R, L and C behave under alternating current, impedance and resonance, the plot of V against I and the three powers. All animated, with exercises at the end.
